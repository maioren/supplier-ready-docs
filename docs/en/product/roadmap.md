# What we're building

We're building Supplier Ready in layers, starting with the problem everything else depends on: understanding with confidence what a new customer requires from your company.

## Understand the requirements

Turn what the customer sent into clear, traceable requirements without silent assumptions.

## Understand your company

Relate those requirements to your company's reality: what already exists, what is valid, and what needs attention.

## Identify the gaps

Show what is missing, what needs action, and what depends on clarification.

## Get to Ready

Create an objective view of readiness so your company knows when it is prepared to move forward.
