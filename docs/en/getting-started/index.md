# Getting Started

## From what your customer requires to what your company needs to do.

Supplier Ready helps you turn a new customer's requirements into a clear path to **Ready**.

The process starts by understanding what was requested, moves through comparing those requirements with your company's reality, and ends with an objective view of what is resolved, what still requires action, and what needs clarification.

## The path to Ready

### 1. Create an analysis

Each new process starts with an **analysis** linked to a customer's requirements.

### 2. Bring in the requirements

Provide the content received from the customer so Supplier Ready can begin identifying what is being requested.

### 3. Review the interpretation

See the identified requirements, their conditions, and the evidence in the original source.

### 4. Clarify what is uncertain

When a requirement depends on context or cannot be determined safely, Supplier Ready makes that uncertainty explicit.

### 5. Compare with your company

Check which requirements your company already meets and which still require action.

### 6. Resolve the gaps

Work through what is missing, needs attention, or still depends on clarification.

### 7. Reach Ready

When everything that needs to be resolved has been resolved, your company is **Ready** to move forward with that customer.

Supplier Ready is under development. This documentation distinguishes capabilities already validated in the **MVP engine** from the integrated product experience we're still building.
