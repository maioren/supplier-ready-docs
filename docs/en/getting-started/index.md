# Getting Started

Supplier Ready helps your company turn a new customer's requirements into a structured view of what needs to be satisfied.

The flow we're building is simple: bring the requirements, understand them with traceability, compare them with your company's reality, clarify uncertainty, and identify what's missing to get **Ready**.

## What you'll find here

These docs describe the public direction of the product. Because Supplier Ready is still in development, some experiences may evolve as they are validated.
