# Your first analysis

!!! info "In development"
    This page describes the experience we're building and will be updated as the full flow becomes available.

An analysis begins with the requirements received from your customer.

## 1. Bring the source

Provide the text or document you received. Supplier Ready treats the original source as evidence of what was actually requested.

## 2. Review identified requirements

Requirements are organized into individual, traceable items.

## 3. Clarify uncertainty

When a conclusion cannot be safely supported by the source, the uncertainty stays visible instead of becoming an assumption.

## 4. Compare with your company

From the requirements, the product can show what is already met, what needs action, and what still needs clarification.

## 5. Get to Ready

The goal is to turn requirements into a clear path for your company to move forward with the new customer.
