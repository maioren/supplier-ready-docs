# Guides

## Complete a specific task in Supplier Ready.

Guides are action-oriented. They distinguish what is available in the current product journey, what exists only in the MVP engine, and what is still being developed as an integrated product experience.

- [Create an analysis](create-analysis.md) — available in the current journey.
- [Review interpreted requirements](review-interpreted-requirements.md) — available in the current journey.
- [Resolve clarifications](resolve-clarifications.md) — available in the current journey.
- [Assess a requirement](assess-requirement.md) — domain states exist; complete evidence/assessment experience is in development.
- [Understand and resolve gaps](resolve-gaps.md) — product direction; `Gap` is not yet a formal domain object.
- [Track Readiness](track-readiness.md) — deterministic calculation exists in the engine; integrated tracking experience is in development.

> **A guide describes what you can actually do—not an interface that does not exist yet.**
